How NEET PG Counseling Works

1. The Two Quota System

All India Quota (AIQ) — 50% of government seats: Conducted by the Medical Counseling Committee (MCC) for all MD/MS/Diploma seats surrendered to the central pool by states, plus all seats in central institutes and deemed/private universities.

State Quota — remaining 50% of government seats + state private seats: Conducted separately by each state's counseling authority (e.g. DGHS/DME of that state), with its own rules, reservation categories, and domicile requirements.

You can register for both AIQ and your home state's counseling at the same time — they run on independent, overlapping timelines.

2. Typical Round Structure

  1. Round 1 — First allotment based on choices, rank, and category. You must report and either join or exercise "float"/upgrade options as per that year's rules.
  2. Round 2 — Candidates already allotted a seat can try to upgrade; new candidates can enter with fresh choices against vacant seats.
  3. Mop-Up Round — For remaining vacancies after Round 2; typically the last MCC-conducted round for many quotas.
  4. Stray Vacancy Round — Fills seats still vacant after Mop-Up, sometimes conducted on-the-spot or with compressed timelines. Rules on withdrawal/joining are usually stricter here.

Exact round names, count, and rules change year to year and differ between AIQ and individual states — always check the current year's official information bulletin.

3. Choice Filling & Locking

  • List colleges/courses in genuine order of preference — allotment is rank + choice driven, so listing a seat doesn't force you to take it if you rank higher on a preferred choice.
  • Some rounds require you to explicitly "lock" your choices before a deadline — an unlocked list may be auto-locked by the system or may not be considered, depending on that year's rule.
  • Research course + college together — a "good college, weak department" or vice versa can matter more than brand name for your specialty.

4. Reporting & Joining

  • After allotment, you typically report to the allotted college (or do online reporting, depending on quota/state) within a fixed window, with fee payment and document verification.
  • Failing to report/join without valid upgradation intent can lead to seat forfeiture and, in many states, a period of debarment from future counseling — check current penalty rules.
  • If you intend to try for an upgrade in a later round, you generally must still join and pay initial dues for your current allotment to keep that seat safe while you wait.

5. Documents Usually Required

  • NEET PG admit card & rank letter
  • MBBS/BDS degree certificate & mark sheets
  • Internship completion certificate (or expected date proof)
  • Permanent/provisional medical registration certificate
  • Category certificate (OBC-NCL / SC / ST / EWS / PwD) valid for the counseling year
  • Domicile certificate (for state quota)
  • Valid photo ID, passport-size photographs, counseling fee receipt

Requirements vary by state and quota — always follow the exact checklist in that year's information bulletin.
